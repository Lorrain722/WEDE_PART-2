About eKofini@Proper

eKofini@Proper is a locally owned coffee shop serving the Ekangala community with fresh coffee, meals and snacks.

We provide a warm and welcoming environment where customers can enjoy quality food and drinks while spending time with family, friends, students, professionals and visitors.

Our goal is to provide excellent customer service while creating a comfortable space where members of the community can meet, relax and enjoy good food.

Our Story

eKofini@Proper was established to provide the local community with a convenient place to enjoy fresh coffee, tasty meals and snacks.

Over time, the coffee shop has become more than just a place to buy food and drinks. It provides a welcoming community space for meetings, family time, studying, conversations and social gatherings.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve fresh, affordable and quality food and beverages while providing friendly customer service in a welcoming environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted community coffee shop that brings people together through quality food, great coffee and excellent service.
